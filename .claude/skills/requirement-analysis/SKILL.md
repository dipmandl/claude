# Requirement Analysis Skill

## Objective

Convert a business request into implementation-ready requirements.

## Process

1. Identify business objective.
2. Identify users and actors.
3. Identify functional requirements.
4. Identify non-functional requirements.
5. Identify business rules.
6. Identify dependencies.
7. Identify assumptions.
8. Identify risks.
9. Identify acceptance criteria.
10. Identify open questions.

## Requirement Quality

Every requirement should be:

- Specific
- Testable
- Unambiguous
- Traceable
- Feasible

## Acceptance Criteria

Use Given/When/Then where appropriate.

Example:

Given an authenticated customer

When the customer submits a valid order

Then the order should be created successfully.

## Output

Create:

.sdlc/requirements.md

Do not implement code.
